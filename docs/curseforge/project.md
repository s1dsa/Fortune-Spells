# Meet Your Fight: Iron's Spells Expansion

Turn Meet Your Fight boss trophies into 13 spells for Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, then forge and name a staff at a thirteen-seal altar.

## From a boss fight to your spellbook

Beat Dame Fortuna, take her Fortune's Favor to a Scroll Forge, and craft **Fate's Choice**. Five cards appear in front of you. Keep your eye on the marked card as they shuffle, then aim at it to make your pick.

Each of the four bosses brings a different kind of magic. **Phantom Belfry** summons a bell that strikes the battlefield with sound. **Undead Devour** draws creatures into a spectral crocodile's jaws. **Sunset Blade Dance** scatters petals with each swing and ignites them on the final stroke. **Bone Wings** gives you flight while its reserve of grey hearts lasts.

## Forge a staff of your own

Pour four bottles of **Transcendent Ink** into an **Alchemist Cauldron**, then throw in a trophy from any one of the four bosses. After about five seconds, use the cauldron with an empty hand to collect the **Audience** scroll.

The scroll opens an altar after a base chant of 35 seconds. Place eight bottles of ink on the outer seals, one trophy from each boss on the four inner seals, and **The Blood Staff** from the Dead King in the centre. The altar consumes one mana per second while active. When the offerings converge, pick up the new staff and give it a name. Its spell wheel carries four level-10 Transcendent spells and **Cataclysm**, a staff-exclusive spell that sends a beam through nine rings. Press **I** during a staff cast to fly the camera around the ritual, or **U** to return to your character.

## Installation

For **Minecraft Java Edition 1.20.1**, **Forge 47.4.0 or later in the 47.x line**, and **Java 17**. Install on both the client and the server. The required mods are Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Meet Your Fight, Curios API and GeckoLib. For a manual installation with Iron's Spells 3.16.4, also install Iron's Lib and playerAnimator.

## 中文介绍

**迎战铁魔法联动（铁魔法扩展）**把《迎战》四位首领的招式带进铁魔法的卷轴和法术书，共有 13 个法术。打败福尔图娜女爵后，用她掉落的命运之眷制作「命运之选」。五张牌在你眼前亮出、翻面、洗开，用准星选中记住的那张牌，就能获得命运的祝福。

四个超位法术各有用途。幽钟齐鸣召出悬钟，以钟声攻击周围的生物；亡鳄吞噬把目标持续拉进巨口；落日剑舞每斩散出花瓣，收招时一并引爆；骨翼则让你在灰心耗尽前飞行。

想给自己的法杖命名，先向炼金釜倒入四瓶超位墨水，再投入四位首领中任意一位的掉落物。约五秒后空手右键取出「觐见」卷轴。基础吟唱 35 秒后，外圈八个阵位各放一瓶超位墨水，内圈四个阵位各放一种首领掉落物，阵心放入死者之王的猩红神杖。祭坛每秒消耗 1 点法力。拾起铸好的法杖即可取名；杖上自带四个 10 级超位法术和专属的九环「爆裂魔法」。持杖吟唱时，按 **I** 自由观察法阵，按 **U** 收回镜头。

支持 Minecraft 1.20.1、Forge 47.4.0 及以上的 47.x 版本、Java 17。联机时，客户端和服务端都要安装本模组、铁魔法、迎战、Curios API 和 GeckoLib。手动安装铁魔法 3.16.4 时，还需安装 Iron's Lib 和 playerAnimator。
